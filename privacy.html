Privacy Policy

Welcome to our website! Below you’ll find information about our privacy practices and how we handle your data. Feel free to browse all pages on our site without restrictions.

Website Information

Our website address is: https://www.davrentrucking.com.

Cookies

If you leave a comment, you may choose to save your name, email address, and website in cookies for convenience. This allows you to avoid re-entering your details for future comments. These cookies will remain active for one year.

If you visit our login page, a temporary cookie will be created to check if your browser accepts cookies. This cookie does not contain personal data and will be deleted when you close your browser.

Embedded Content

Some pages or articles on our site may include embedded content (e.g., videos, images, articles, etc.). Embedded content from external websites behaves as if you visited those websites directly. These websites may collect your data, use cookies, embed third-party tracking, and monitor your interaction with the content if you are logged into their site.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. If you request a password reset, your IP address will be included in the reset email. Any information you provide through contact forms, employment forms, or quote requests will be used solely to process your inquiry and respond to your messages.

Data Retention

Comments and their metadata are retained indefinitely to streamline the approval of follow-up comments. This helps us avoid placing them in moderation queues.

Your Data Rights

If you have an account on our site or have left comments, you can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you. Additionally, you may request that we erase your personal data. This excludes any information we are required to retain for administrative, legal, or security purposes.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, feel free to contact us.
